    How to read a match and choose your next step

    Compare the matching skills and requirements. “Partial JD” means a summary only; add the full description before tailoring. AI fit considers role, skills and experience; it is not your chance of being hired.

    Use Shortlist to save a job in Step 3, or Select & tailor resume to start reviewing a draft.

    Find and add current jobs from Naukri

    Naukri opens in a new tab. Jobs appear in this app after you add their descriptions or confirm a browser import.

    1. Open a search below. Choose an area. Check the experience and other filters on Naukri.
    2. Choose a relevant job on Naukri. Copy its title, company, location, page link and complete job description.
    3. Add it here. Click the button below, paste the details and choose Save job & review match.

    Add a job description

    Paste the details from Naukri or an employer’s job page. Saving a job does not submit an application.

    Import jobs from your Naukri browser (optional helper)

    Use this to import several visible job cards. Start with “Add a job description” above if you prefer to skip extension setup.

    1. In Chrome, enter chrome://extensions in the address bar. For Edge, use edge://extensions.
    2. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and select D:\BadekarEnterprises\JobApplier\browser-helper.
    3. Open a Naukri results page normally. Click the browser’s Extensions icon, open the helper and click Capture job data.
    4. Choose Open Job Applier with these jobs. Use the same local app address and browser as this tab, then review and confirm the import here.
    5. For a complete description, open the full job page on Naukri and capture it again. The same job link updates the saved entry.

    The Naukri page must already be viewable. The helper reads job fields only when you click Capture; it does not read cookies or passwords.
